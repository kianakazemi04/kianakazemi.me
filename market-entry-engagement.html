Who I Am

01

Business Consultant. Istanbul-based. Global focus.

I work at the intersection of market intelligence and business development — helping companies understand a new market before they enter it, structure their entry correctly, and build their digital presence in that market from day one. My focus is the Turkey-to-Dubai and Turkey-to-Europe corridor.

I don't run agencies. I don't deliver reports that sit on a shelf. I work on one thing: making the first move into a new market intelligent.

What I Do

02

Three Areas. One Objective: Your First Move Works.

Important: Legal, tax and regulatory services are provided exclusively by verified third-party partners in the target market. I act as strategic advisor and coordinator only — ensuring you receive qualified local expertise for every compliance requirement.

01 / 03Before You Commit Capital

Market Intelligence & Feasibility

A clear picture of what the market actually looks like — competitive landscape, buyer behavior, pricing dynamics, and whether there's a realistic path to revenue.

What You Receive
  • Competitive landscape analysis (top 5–8 players)
  • Buyer behavior mapping & pricing benchmarks
  • Go / No-Go framework with rationale
  • Written report (15–20 pages) + review call
02 / 03Structure the Move

Entry Strategy & First 90 Days

Your complete entry roadmap — legal structure (through local partners), channel strategy, ICP definition for the new market, and a 90-day action plan.

What You Receive
  • Market entry sequence & legal entity recommendation
  • ICP definition & channel strategy for the new market
  • 90-day action plan with decision checkpoints
  • Risk mapping & mitigation approach
03 / 03Be Found First

Digital Presence in the New Market

When you enter a new market, the first thing a partner or customer does is search for you. Digital infrastructure is built by the Digital Growth Engine team, coordinated under my market entry strategy.

What You Receive
  • Localized landing page or website (language + SEO)
  • LinkedIn & social presence setup for the new market
  • 60-day content foundation & SEO baseline
  • Outreach messaging framework

How I Work

03

Four Steps. No Surprises.

  1. Step 01 · Free

    Discovery Call

    30 minutes. No commitment. I ask the right questions to understand your situation. If there's no fit, I'll tell you directly.

  2. Step 02 · 1–2 Days

    Scope Agreement

    I define exactly what the engagement covers — deliverables, timeline, and what falls outside scope. Written. Clear. No ambiguity.

  3. Step 03 · 2–4 Weeks

    Delivery

    Work begins. Every engagement produces written, structured deliverables — not just conversation. Interim updates for longer engagements.

  4. Step 04 · Included

    Review & Next Steps

    Final delivery call. We review everything together and agree on what comes next — a second phase, a partner introduction, or moving forward independently.

Right Fit

04

Who This Is For — and Who It Isn't

This Is for You If…

  • You have an e-commerce brand, SaaS product, or B2B service with proven revenue in Turkey
  • You want to enter Dubai/UAE or a European market but don't know where to start
  • You want to understand the market before committing capital — not after
  • You need both strategy and digital execution support — not just a report
  • You are a founder or decision-maker, not looking for junior execution only

This Is NOT for You If…

  • You need legal advice or tax consulting (I can refer you to the right partner)
  • You are a traditional manufacturer looking for import/export logistics only
  • You want a contact list or cold introductions without strategic context
  • Your company has no existing revenue or validated product
