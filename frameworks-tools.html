Business Strategy

Frameworks used to define direction, structure and long-term positioning.

Tools Often Used
  • Excel
  • Notion
  • Miro
  • Claude

Business Model Framework

Maps how the business creates, delivers and captures value.

When to use:

Early-stage design or business model review.

Business Model Assessment

Evaluates the strengths and weaknesses of an existing model.

When to use:

Before a growth or transformation decision.

Value Proposition Framework

Aligns customer pains and gains with product benefits.

When to use:

Positioning or messaging is unclear.

Revenue Architecture

Structures how multiple revenue streams work together.

When to use:

Diversifying or redesigning monetization.

Growth Roadmap

Sequences strategic priorities across a defined time horizon.

When to use:

Translating strategy into a phased plan.

Strategic Prioritization Matrix

Ranks initiatives by impact versus effort or feasibility.

When to use:

Too many competing priorities exist.

Business Capability Assessment

Evaluates whether internal capabilities support the strategy.

When to use:

Before committing to an ambitious plan.

Current-State Assessment

Establishes a factual baseline before recommending change.

When to use:

At the start of nearly every engagement.

Gap Analysis

Compares current state against the desired future state.

When to use:

Identifying what must change to progress.

Executive Decision Framework

Structures how leadership evaluates and commits to decisions.

When to use:

Decision quality or speed is inconsistent.

Growth Strategy

  • Excel
  • Notion
  • Google Analytics

Growth Opportunity Matrix

Multiple growth paths need comparison.

Growth Constraint Analysis

When to use:

Growth Horizon Framework

Separates near, mid and long-term growth bets.

When to use:

Balancing today's execution with tomorrow's bets.

Opportunity Prioritization Matrix

Scores opportunities against strategic fit and resource cost.

When to use:

Allocating limited growth budget.

Capability Roadmap

Sequences the capabilities required to support growth.

When to use:

Growth is outpacing internal readiness.

Strategic Initiative Portfolio

Views all active initiatives as a balanced portfolio.

When to use:

Too many parallel initiatives with unclear ROI.

Resource Allocation Matrix

Aligns budget and headcount with strategic priority.

When to use:

Resources are spread too thin.

KPI Strategy Framework

Defines which metrics actually indicate strategic progress.

When to use:

Teams are tracking metrics that don't matter.

  • HubSpot CRM
  • Figma
  • Google Analytics

GTM Strategy Canvas

ICP Framework

Buyer Persona Framework

Messaging isn't resonating with buyers.

Customer Journey Mapping

When to use:

Value Messaging Framework

Structures messaging around outcomes, not features.

When to use:

Messaging feels feature-heavy or generic.

Positioning Matrix

Maps the business against competitors on key value dimensions.

When to use:

Differentiation is unclear in a crowded market.

Pricing Strategy Framework

Aligns pricing structure with customer-perceived value.

When to use:

Pricing was set by guesswork or competitors.

Sales Motion Architecture

Defines the repeatable steps from lead to closed deal.

When to use:

Sales depends entirely on individual reps.

Commercialization Framework

Structures how a new offering moves from concept to market.

When to use:

Launching a new product or service line.

Channel Strategy

Evaluates and prioritizes acquisition and distribution channels.

When to use:

Uncertain where to invest acquisition budget.

Partner Strategy

Structures how partnerships extend reach and value.

When to use:

Direct acquisition alone isn't enough.

Route-to-Market Framework

Defines the operational path products take to reach customers.

When to use:

Entering a new segment or geography.

Market Research & Expansion

Frameworks used to evaluate markets, competitors and expansion decisions.

Tools Often Used
  • Statista
  • Crunchbase
  • Google Trends
  • CB Insights

TAM

Estimates the total market demand for a category.

When to use:

Sizing the overall opportunity.

SAM

Narrows TAM to the segment the business can realistically serve.

When to use:

Defining a practical addressable market.

SOM

Estimates the obtainable share within a defined time frame.

When to use:

Setting realistic near-term targets.

Market Growth Analysis

Evaluates the trajectory and drivers of market expansion.

When to use:

Judging long-term market attractiveness.

Market Attractiveness Matrix

Scores markets by size, growth and competitive intensity.

When to use:

Comparing multiple market options.

Customer Segmentation

Groups customers by shared needs and behaviors.

When to use:

One-size-fits-all targeting is failing.

Competitor Mapping

Catalogs direct and indirect competitors and their positioning.

When to use:

Entering or operating in a crowded space.

Competitive Landscape

Synthesizes competitor strengths, weaknesses and white space.

When to use:

Deciding where to differentiate.

Porter's Five Forces

Assesses industry attractiveness through five competitive forces.

When to use:

Evaluating structural industry risk.

PESTLE Analysis

Scans political, economic, social, tech, legal and environmental factors.

When to use:

Assessing macro-level market risk.

SWOT

Summarizes internal strengths/weaknesses and external opportunities/threats.

When to use:

A fast, structured strategic snapshot.

Entry Barrier Analysis

Identifies obstacles to entering a new market.

When to use:

Assessing market entry difficulty.

Regulatory Assessment

Evaluates legal and compliance requirements for a market.

When to use:

Entering a regulated industry or region.

Country Attractiveness Matrix

Ranks countries by opportunity and ease of entry.

When to use:

Prioritizing among multiple expansion targets.

Entry Mode Framework

Evaluates direct, partnership or acquisition entry models.

When to use:

Deciding how, not just where, to enter.

Scenario Planning

Models multiple plausible futures to stress-test a decision.

When to use:

High uncertainty around key assumptions.

Risk & Decision

Frameworks used to evaluate uncertainty and structure hard decisions.

Tools Often Used
  • Excel
  • Miro

Risk Heat Map

Visualizes risks by likelihood and potential impact.

When to use:

Prioritizing which risks need attention first.

Strategic Risk Assessment

Identifies risks that could undermine long-term strategy.

When to use:

Before committing to an aggressive growth plan.

Trade-Off Matrix

Compares options across competing decision criteria.

When to use:

No option is clearly superior on every dimension.

Decision Tree

Maps decision paths and their probable outcomes.

When to use:

A decision has several sequential branches.

Scenario Analysis

Tests a decision against best-, base- and worst-case conditions.

When to use:

Outcomes depend on uncertain external factors.

Sensitivity Analysis

Tests how sensitive an outcome is to key assumptions.

When to use:

One or two assumptions carry outsized weight.

Assumption Validation

Tests whether the assumptions behind a plan actually hold.

When to use:

A strategy rests on unverified beliefs.

Proprietary

KK Proprietary Frameworks

Structures I've developed by combining and adapting established strategic theory into models built specifically around how founders and growing businesses actually make decisions.

Diagnosis

KK Strategic Diagnosis™

A structured first-pass diagnostic that identifies the true constraint behind a business problem before recommending any solution.

Built from Gap Analysis, Current-State Assessment and root-cause thinking.

When to use:

The start of nearly every engagement.

Growth

KK Growth Constraint Matrix™

Pinpoints which single factor is actually limiting growth, rather than treating every growth lever as equally important.

Built from Growth Constraint Analysis and Theory of Constraints thinking.

When to use:

Growth has plateaued despite continued effort.

Commercial

KK Commercialization Engine™

A repeatable model connecting ICP, positioning, pricing and sales motion into one commercial system rather than separate decisions.

Built from GTM Strategy Canvas, Sales Motion Architecture and Pricing Strategy.

When to use:

Commercial functions operate in isolation from each other.

Marketplace

KK Trust Architecture™

A framework for designing the specific mechanisms that build confidence between two parties in a transaction.

Built from Trust Architecture Framework and Buyer-Seller Interaction Model.

When to use:

Trust, not demand, is the barrier to conversion.

Enterprise

KK Enterprise Adoption Model™

Maps how a new capability actually spreads through a large organization, from first champion to habitual use.

Built from the Digital Adoption Curve and Enterprise Decision Journey.

When to use:

Enterprise adoption is slower than expected.

Expansion

KK International Expansion Engine™

A structured sequence for evaluating, entering and scaling within a new geographic market with minimized downside risk.

Built from Country Attractiveness Matrix, Entry Mode Framework and Scenario Planning.

When to use:

Considering entry into Turkey, MENA or another new market.

Prioritization

KK Strategic Priority Matrix™

Forces a single ranked list of priorities rather than a flat backlog everything competes equally within.

Built from Strategic Prioritization Matrix and Resource Allocation Matrix.

When to use:

Leadership has too many "top priorities" at once.

Value

KK Value Realization Blueprint™

Tracks whether the value promised in a strategy or business case is actually being delivered post-implementation.

Built from the Value Realization Framework and KPI Strategy Framework.

When to use:

Confirming a strategic initiative delivered what it promised.
