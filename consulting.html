Two Strategic Lenses. One Objective.

Business Strategy vs. Growth Strategy

These two are often used interchangeably — but they answer very different questions, and mixing them up leads to unfocused engagements.

Business Strategy

What business should we build, and where should we go?

Business Strategy focuses on the direction the business itself should take.

Covers
  • Business Model
  • Strategic Direction
  • Positioning
  • Strategic Priorities
  • Growth Constraints
  • Long-Term Roadmap
  • Capability Development
  • Execution Priorities
Typical Deliverables

Business Growth Roadmap · Business Model Strategy & Optimization

Growth Strategy

How do we acquire more customers and grow faster?

Growth Strategy focuses on how the existing business grows.

Covers
  • Customer Acquisition
  • ICP & Market Segmentation
  • Positioning & Acquisition Channels
  • Sales Strategy
  • Growth Opportunities
  • Revenue Expansion
  • Funnel Optimization
  • Growth Roadmap
Typical Deliverables

Go-To-Market Strategy · Growth Opportunity & Revenue Expansion Strategy

Business Strategy defines the direction. Growth Strategy defines how the business scales.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who do you typically work with?

Founders, business owners and leadership teams at startups, SMEs and growing technology companies.

What industries do you focus on?

Primarily AI, B2B SaaS, PropTech, technology and professional services — though the frameworks apply broadly.

How does the consulting process begin?

With a discovery call to understand your business and challenge, followed by a proposed scope and approach.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. Engagements are conducted remotely, with calls and workshops scheduled around your timezone.

Do you offer ongoing advisory?

Yes, through the Strategic Advisor and Monthly Retainer engagement models.

How are consulting engagements structured?

Scope, timeline and deliverables are agreed upfront based on the engagement model that fits your situation best.
